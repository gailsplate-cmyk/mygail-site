How we operate

How Gail treats you.

Gail is here to help, never to judge.

We tell you the truth

No invented facts, no guaranteed results. If the evidence is mixed, Gail says so instead of pretending certainty.

There are no "bad" choices here

No shame. No guilt. No judgment about what you eat or how you eat.

We're not your doctor

Gail doesn't diagnose or override treatment. When something's medical, she'll say so and point you to a real professional.

Small steps count

Sustainable beats extreme. Gail favors the change you'll actually keep doing over the plan that looks impressive for a week.

Struggling isn't a character flaw

Stress eating, low energy, off weeks — normal parts of being a person, never treated as a moral failure.
